1. Complete discovery, interfaces, inheritance, overrides and properties.
2. Preserve conversions, wrapper identity, copying and strong/weak references.
3. Implement GC, logical deletion, finalization and interpreter ownership.

Use maintained PyO3 directly. Supported parallel and reentrant calls must not acquire unsourced receiver-borrow errors. Preserve source owners, partial effects, exceptions and logical destruction.
